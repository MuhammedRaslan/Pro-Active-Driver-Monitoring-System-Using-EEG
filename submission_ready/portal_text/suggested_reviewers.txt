Suggested reviewers

The IEEE Sensors Journal portal will request 3–5 suggested reviewers and 0–2 reviewers to exclude. Below is a list of credible candidates plus the rationale for each suggestion. Pick 4 from the SUGGEST list and 1 from the EXCLUDE list when filling out the portal.

Rules to follow before final selection:
  - Do not suggest anyone from VIT (any campus) or anyone you have co-authored with.
  - Verify each candidate is currently active in EEG / drowsiness / driver-monitoring research (Google Scholar shows recent papers in 2023–2025).
  - Confirm institutional email is current via the candidate's faculty page before listing.
  - The portal sometimes auto-checks for conflicts; if it flags anyone, drop them silently and pick from the alternates.
